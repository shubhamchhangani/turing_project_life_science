- Lineage photon inference: Claude Opus 5.5 low, reward 1, 49.361426 seconds.

Both are normal substantive solves. GPT was not launched after either result.

What failed in task design:
- Large planar cores still admit Pfaffian reductions and efficient recursive
  evaluation. Adding a finite exceptional-site set did not defeat this method.
- Integrated exposure and branching inheritance look complicated but reduce to
  a small augmented sparse generator followed by exact message contraction.
  Claude derived and coded this complete solution in two commands.
- Scientific validity and comprehensive tests are necessary but do not establish
  model difficulty. Neither trial exposes a verifier loophole.

Requirements for the next candidate:
- Establish a genuine inverse/reconstruction or inference bottleneck rather than
  counting dimensions, narrative length, or a longer list of outputs.
- First demonstrate a genuine author solution and independent scientific checks.
- A new problem must remain finite, explicit and solvable. A currently unsolved
  research question does not supply a verifiable binary answer automatically.
- Do not hide requirements, tighten tolerances without numerical justification,
  exploit environment failures, or change expected values to manufacture failure.
- Claude first. GPT only after substantive Claude failure. ZIP only after both
  normal failures and all scientific, format and isolated-verifier checks.

There is no evidence permitting a guarantee that every frontier model will fail.
No model trial is currently running.
